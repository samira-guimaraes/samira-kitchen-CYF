Chocolate Cake

⏱ 1 hour · 🍽 8 servings

A soft, moist sponge topped with a rich chocolate ganache — the perfect dessert for any occasion.

Ingredients

  • 200g plain flour
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 50g cocoa powder
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • 200ml milk
  • 100ml sunflower oil
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 100g dark chocolate, melted (for the ganache)
  • 100ml double cream (for the ganache)

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (fan 160°C) and grease a 20cm round cake tin.
  2. In a large bowl, sieve together the flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking powder and salt.
  3. In a separate jug, whisk the eggs, milk, sunflower oil and vanilla extract.
  4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and mix until smooth.
  5. Pour the batter into the tin and bake for 30–35 minutes, or until a skewer comes out clean.
  6. Leave the cake to cool completely on a wire rack before adding the ganache.
  7. To make the ganache, warm the double cream gently and pour it over the melted dark chocolate, stirring until glossy.
  8. Spread the ganache over the cooled cake and leave to set for 15 minutes before serving.
